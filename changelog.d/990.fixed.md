- **`am`'s trajectory outputs cover every experiment, are normalized once, and are refused
  where they would be wrong (#990).**
  - An experiment under a `condition:`, or an edition-1 mutant, got no `output_trajectory` or
    `output_noise_trajectory` file, with no message. It now gets its files.
  - With `normalization`, the run stopped at its first accepted result (`ValueError`).
    `output_trajectory` records the normalized series the objective scores. An
    `output_noise_trajectory` column under normalization, and either output on a `scale`
    column, are refused when the configuration loads.
  - A misspelled name is refused when the configuration loads, with the names it could match.
  - `normalization = scale` on a noise model with no additive scale (`neg_bin`) is refused
    when the configuration loads, instead of failing every evaluation.
  - `am` no longer stops when a chain's first simulation fails, or under
    `local_objective_eval = 1` or `smoothing > 1`.
  - `-r` no longer stops with `IndexError` when an `mh` or `pt` chain had no result at the
    backup it resumes from.
