- **A `neg_bin` noise model scores a large dispersion for the right mean.** The score clipped
  `r/(r + m)` at `1 - 1e-10`, a floor of `r × 1e-10` on the expected count that grew with the
  dispersion. From about `r = 1e7` on, every expected count below it was scored as the floor: at
  `fix_at 1e12` a zero prediction made a zero count cost 100, and counts of a few per interval
  were all scored as Poisson(100). The floor is now capped at `1e-7`, and a dispersion above
  about `1e3` is scored without forming `r/(r + m)`, accurate to `1e-12` at `r = 1e12`. A score
  at a dispersion up to about `1e3` is unchanged to the bit. `job_type = lwf` now takes a
  dispersion up to `1e12`, where its forecast draw is still right, instead of `1e6`.
