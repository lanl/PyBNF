- **On bngsim, an action after a `simulate` that stops early starts where BNG2.pl's does
  (#1010).** bngsim checks `stop_if` only after integrating the whole span, so the bridge
  carried the state at `t_end` into every later action, and its clock read `t_end` after a
  `stop_if` or `steady_state` stop: a treat-below-a-threshold model scored 502548 where BNG2.pl
  scores 0.07. The bridge now puts every species back at the row the run stopped at, and the
  clock at its time, as BNG2.pl reads them back from its last row. A gradient fit that
  continues from such a stop is refused, since the derivatives cannot be put back there.
  `stop_if` on `ssa` or `psa`, which BNG2.pl checks after every reaction and so stops between
  output times, is handed to BNG2.pl under `bngl_backend = auto` and refused under `bngsim`.
